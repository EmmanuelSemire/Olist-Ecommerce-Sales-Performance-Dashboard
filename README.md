# Olist Brazilian E-Commerce: Sales Performance & Revenue Dashboard

An executive-level Power BI analytics dashboard built to analyze Olist's e-commerce operations, tracking over **99K orders**, **$13.59M+ in total revenue**, logistics lead times, and seller concentration metrics.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Insights
* **Sales & Revenue Analysis:** Evaluates revenue trends, top customer states, payment method breakdowns, and average order values ($136.68).
* **Delivery & Logistics Performance:** Analyzes average shipping times, freight shares (16.57%), late delivery rates (8.1%), and regional bottlenecks across Brazilian states.
* **Seller & Product Concentration:** Implements **Pareto (80/20) tier analysis** to identify top-performing product categories and high-impact active sellers.
* **Custom UI/UX Design:** Features a custom-built, minimalist flat-vector icon suite styled in a dark burgundy and burnt-orange executive SaaS aesthetic.
